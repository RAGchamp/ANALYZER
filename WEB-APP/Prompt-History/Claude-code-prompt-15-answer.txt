# Bharat Forge (Consolidated FY2025-26): what the material says about the commercial vehicles business

## Summary
- **No commercial vehicle (CV) figures are in the material I was given.** The question asks for management's discussion, but no MD&A or other report passages were sent. The notes and statements never mention "commercial vehicles", CV volumes, or a split between CV and passenger vehicles. I cannot give CV revenue, margins or market commentary without inventing them.
- **The closest stand-in is the Forgings segment.** It covers forgings and machined parts for the **automotive and industrial sectors** (Note 49, p.472), so CV is somewhere inside it. External revenue rose **7.5%** to ₹139,285.82m. The segment result rose **5.4%** to ₹19,560.91m, so the margin slipped from 14.3% to 14.0% (Note 49, p.473).
- **Forgings grew more slowly than the Group** (Group revenue +11.2%). Its share of gross segment revenue fell from 82.6% to 78.0% as "Others" more than doubled.
- **Forgings took its first exceptional loss** (₹1,073.54m against nil the year before). It also raised capital spending 26.7% to ₹18,879.40m, and its pre-tax return on capital employed fell slightly, from 17.9% to 17.2%.
- **Revenue by region shifted towards India (+43.2%) and Europe (+19.0%). The US was nearly flat (+3.4%) and "Other" regions fell 34.9%** (Note 49, p.475). These figures are for the whole Group, not for CV.

## Detailed analysis

### 1. Where the CV business sits
- Note 1 (p.348) says the Group makes forged and machined components, including aluminium castings, "for the auto and industrial sectors", plus electric-vehicle components and defence products.
- Note 49 (p.472) puts all automotive and industrial forgings in **Forgings**. CV is therefore inside Forgings, together with passenger vehicles and industrial customers.
- The electric-vehicle and other new initiatives are probably in **Others**, but the note does not say so explicitly.
- No product-line revenue is disclosed. Note 24 only splits revenue by type (goods, tooling, job work, scrap, export incentives), not by end market.

### 2. Forgings performance (the proxy)
- **Growth:** external revenue grew 7.46%, compared with 11.17% for the Group. Forgings' share of gross segment revenue:
  - FY26: 139,285.82 ÷ 178,555.66 = **78.0%**
  - FY25: 129,612.33 ÷ 156,852.15 = **82.6%**
- **Margin:** 14.04% against 14.32%. Profit grew more slowly than revenue (+5.4% vs +7.5%).
  - At Group level, raw materials rose from 42.6% of revenue (64,396.23 ÷ 151,228.03) to 46.3% (77,779.31 ÷ 168,116.53) (P&L p.342).
  - That fits with margin pressure, but it is not broken down by segment.
- **Capital employed and return (segment assets less segment liabilities):**
  - FY26: 147,642.44 − 34,064.62 = 113,577.82, so return = 19,560.91 ÷ 113,577.82 = **17.2%**
  - FY25: 132,272.20 − 28,754.18 = 103,518.02, so return = 18,561.18 ÷ 103,518.02 = **17.9%**
- **Investment:** additions of ₹18,879.40m are 2.3 times Forgings depreciation of ₹8,285.96m. The segment is investing for growth, but returns have not yet caught up.
- **Exceptional loss:** ₹1,073.54m in Forgings. Note 32 was not provided, so its nature is unknown.

### Balance Sheet
- Total assets of ₹222,599.55m (BS p.341) equal Note 49's figure after inter-segment eliminations. Forgings accounts for 66% of that total.
- Equity-accounted investments of ₹614.14m equal Note 49 (6.1).
- Working capital that probably relates to Forgings grew: trade receivables rose from 28,996.06 to 39,097.99 (+34.8%) and inventories from 35,784.36 to 40,527.83 (+13.3%). Both grew faster than revenue.
- **Segment "Total liabilities" of ₹49,259.68m does not agree with the Balance Sheet's ₹127,031.31m.** Borrowings, lease and tax balances appear to be left out (see red flags).

### Cash Flow Statement
- Profit before tax (16,671.72), depreciation (9,709.24) and finance costs (3,233.46) link to Note 49 (p.346).
- Operating cash flow fell 17.2% to ₹14,866.42m (from 17,963.43), mainly because receivables rose by ₹7,090.42m.
- Capital expenditure paid was ₹11,404.96m. That is well below the segment asset additions of ₹29,606.73m, which include capital work-in-progress transfers, right-of-use assets and the acquisition.

### Statement of Changes in Equity
- Nothing specific to segments or CV.
- Profit of ₹10,796.61m goes to retained earnings (p.344).
- The cash-flow hedge reserve lost ₹3,973.98m (net movement on hedges ₹(5,312.67)m, P&L p.343). That is relevant to export-heavy forgings but not split by segment.

## Red flags & areas for follow-up
1. **The MD&A is missing.** CV volumes, the India vs North America vs Europe CV cycle, customers and outlook cannot be analysed. Re-run the question with the MD&A or Board's report passages selected.
2. **Segment liabilities do not reconcile.** Note 49 shows ₹49,259.68m against ₹127,031.31m on the Balance Sheet. Its "Net capital employed" (222,599.55 − 49,259.68 = 173,339.87) is therefore not a true equity-plus-debt measure. Yet footnote * says "no other reconciling items". That statement contradicts the figures.
3. **Note 24's reconciliation to contracted price does not reach reported revenue.**
   - FY26: 167,747.14 against 168,116.53 (a gap of 369.39).
   - FY25: 148,328.68 against 151,228.03 (a gap of 2,899.35).
   - The positive "sales returns, discounts, hedging" adjustment of +920.35 is unusual. Ask what it contains.
4. **Forgings exceptional loss of ₹1,073.54m:** what it is, and whether it relates to CV or automotive plants. Note 32 is needed.
5. **"Other" regions revenue fell 34.9% and the US was flat.** Ask which end markets caused this.
6. **Receivables grew 34.8% against 11.2% revenue growth.** Ask about collections and customer mix.
7. **Rounding differences between Note 49 and the P&L** (0.01): revenue in FY25, profit before exceptional items and profit before tax.
8. **Dates:** Note 1 says the statements were authorised on **May 08, 2026**, but the statements are signed **May 07, 2026**.
9. **Cash flow statement:** the foreign currency translation reserve movement (2,205.34) is added to cash in a way that looks unusual. The prior-year closing cash (5,777.75) is also rebuilt to 6,210.35 the same way. Check the presentation.
