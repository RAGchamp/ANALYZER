## Summary

- **The notes do not report an aerospace business.** Note 49 has three reportable segments: Forgings, Defence and Others (Note 49, p.472). The word "aerospace" does not appear in any note or statement provided. No aerospace revenue, profit, assets or order book can be taken from this material. The nearest disclosed figures are the **Defence segment** and some **aero/defence entities** (Aeron Systems, Edgelab, Kalyani Rafael Advanced Systems, BF Elbit, and Zorya Mashproekt, which works on gas-turbine engines). The answer below uses these as proxies. They are **not** an aerospace figure.
- **Defence revenue was flat but its profit fell sharply.** Revenue was ₹17,571.72m against ₹17,719.86m (−0.8%). Segment result fell 56.3%, from ₹1,864.23m to ₹814.97m, so the margin dropped from 10.5% to 4.6% (Note 49, p.473).
- **Defence is putting in money ahead of revenue.** Additions to non-current assets were ₹2,755.64m (+71.1%), depreciation roughly tripled to ₹242.45m, and segment liabilities rose 26.2% (Note 49, pp.473–475).
- **Some segment figures look odd.** Defence tax expense is ₹442.98m on a pre-tax segment result of about ₹771m. Segment liabilities of ₹49,259.68m do not reconcile to Balance Sheet total liabilities of ₹127,031.31m, even though the note says there are no other reconciling items.
- Group growth came from **Forgings (+7.5%)** and **Others (+127.9%)**. The growth in Others is probably linked to the K Drive acquisition, but the note does not say which segment K Drive sits in.

## Detailed analysis

### 1. What the material does and does not show
- The segments are Forgings, Defence and Others. Forged parts used in defence are counted in **Forgings**, not Defence (Note 49, p.472). The Defence segment therefore understates the group's real defence and aero exposure.
- Any aerospace forgings (for example, components sold to aircraft-engine makers) would fall under "industrial" sales in Forgings. They are not disclosed separately. Note 24 has no split of revenue by end-market.

### 2. Defence segment performance
- **Margin squeeze.** Revenue was flat while the result fell by ₹1,049m. Part of the fall is extra depreciation of ₹162m from new capacity. The remaining ≈₹887m points to a weaker product mix, higher costs or provisions. The notes do not give the reason.
- **Return on net segment assets** (result ÷ (assets − liabilities)):
  - FY2026: 814.97 ÷ 7,218.34 = **11.3%**
  - FY2025: 1,864.23 ÷ 7,968.16 = **23.4%**
- **Asset build-up.** Capex of ₹2,755.64m is 15.7% of segment revenue. A year earlier it was 9.1% (1,610.55 ÷ 17,719.86).
- **Liabilities rose faster than assets** (+26.2% against +9.7%). This could mean more customer advances or payables, but the notes do not break it down.

### 3. Aero and defence entities
- **Equity-accounted investments in Defence:** ₹377.40m. This equals Aeron Systems (₹160.83m) plus Edgelab S.p.A. (₹216.57m), so these two associates sit in Defence (Note 6, p.383; Note 49, p.474). Aeron's carrying value rose ₹16.47m. Edgelab's fell ₹17.44m. The notes do not describe what either company does.
- **Kalyani Rafael Advanced Systems (50% NCI):** revenue grew 5.5% and profit after tax rose 53.4%. Its revenue equals 19.1% of Defence segment revenue, but the note does not confirm which segment it belongs to. Operating cash flow was ₹556.23m and investing outflow ₹771.77m, with non-current assets up sharply from ₹99.12m to ₹1,414.18m (Note 37, pp.426–427).
- **BF Elbit Advanced Systems:** made a loss of ₹(24.31)m and has negative equity of ₹(214.92)m.
- **Zorya Mashproekt India:** builds, repairs and overhauls gas-turbine engines. It recorded no activity in FY2026 (revenue nil, against ₹5.90m in FY2025), although the group bought it for nil consideration in January 2024 (Note 46, p.456; Note 37, p.424).

## Red flags & areas for follow-up
1. **No aerospace disclosure.** Ask management for aerospace revenue, margin, order book and capex. Aerospace may sit inside Forgings (industrial) or Defence.
2. **The Defence margin halved** from 10.5% to 4.6% on flat revenue. Ask for the causes: contract losses, provisions, or the cost of ramping up capacity.
3. **Defence tax rate looks too high.** Tax of ₹442.98m on a pre-tax result of ₹771.20m (814.97 − 43.77) is **57.4%**. The FY2025 equivalent was 289.78 ÷ 1,811.23 = 16.0%. Ask how tax is allocated to segments.
4. **Segment liabilities do not reconcile.** Note 49 shows ₹49,259.68m, but total liabilities are ₹127,031.31m. Borrowings of ₹68,746.18m are probably left out. This contradicts footnote * ("no other reconciling items"). The "Net capital employed" figure of ₹173,339.87m is simply total assets minus segment liabilities.
5. **Label problem.** Segment revenue is headed "from external customers", yet it includes ₹10,439.13m of inter-segment revenue.
6. **Rounding differences of ₹0.01** in segment revenue for FY2025 (151,228.04 against 151,228.03), the pre-exceptional profit subtotal (18,216.16 against 18,216.15) and PBT (16,671.73 against 16,671.72).
7. **Zorya (gas turbines) is dormant.** It had nil revenue in FY2026 and BF Elbit has negative equity. Ask about plans for both.
8. **K Drive (Note 46, p.457):** the cash outflow on acquisition is ₹4,986.23m in the Cash Flow Statement but ₹5,540.88m in the note, a gap of ₹554.65m (possibly deferred consideration). The note also has a typo: "₹1,2582.09 million".
