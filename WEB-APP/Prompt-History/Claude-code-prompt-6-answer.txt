# Where to find the bad debt and provision lines

**None of this is in the extract.** I was only given Note 12 (p.388–390), so I can't give you the exact note number or page. Below is where these lines usually appear in an Ind AS consolidated annual report, and how to find them quickly.

## 1. Where to look in the PDF

| Where to look | What to search for | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **"Other expenses" note** (in the P&L notes, usually after finance costs and depreciation) | "Bad debts", "written off", "doubtful debts", "expected credit loss" | P&L charge for the provision and actual write-offs |
| **"Other income" note** | "written back", "no longer required", "reversal" | **Releases** of the allowance. These are often shown here rather than netted in expenses. |
| **Financial instruments / financial risk management note** (credit risk section) | "Movement in", "loss allowance", "reconciliation" | The full roll-forward (opening, charge, utilised, FX, closing), if the company discloses one |
| **Consolidated cash flow statement** (non-cash adjustments to profit before tax) | "Allowance", "provision for doubtful", "bad debts" | A cross-check on the P&L charge or release |

**Placement clues:**
- This extract refers to Note 18 (Borrowings) and Note 48 (Related parties).
- P&L notes normally sit between the balance sheet notes and the disclosure notes. So "Other expenses" is likely numbered somewhere between these two, after p.390.
- This is a typical layout, not something I have confirmed for this report.

## 2. Common line-item wording

- "Provision for doubtful debts and advances (net)" or "Allowance for expected credit loss"
- "Bad debts / advances written off"
- "Less: provision held / utilised". Write-offs are sometimes shown net of the provision already held.
- "Provision for doubtful debts no longer required, written back". This line usually appears in **Other income**.

## 3. Pitfalls to watch

- **Use the consolidated notes, not the standalone ones.** The annual report contains both sets with similar note numbers. Note 12 here is consolidated (p.388), so use the consolidated section.
- **Receivables may be combined with advances.** Lines often cover "debts and advances" together, so the receivables share may not be separable.
- **Write-offs may be netted.** A small "bad debts written off" figure after deducting the provision utilised can hide a larger gross write-off.
- **Releases may be split out.** Check both the Other expenses and Other income notes before concluding there was a net charge or a net release.
- **The roll-forward may not reconcile exactly.** P&L figures include FX and advances, so they won't tie precisely to the ₹25.24 Mn net fall (592.32 → 567.08, Note 12, p.389).
