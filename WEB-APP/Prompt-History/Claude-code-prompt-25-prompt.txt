with the standalone financial statements. The Group's operating businesses are organised and managed separately according to

products and serves different markets.

the Group's businesses not covered in the Forgings and Defence segments.

and industrial sectors and the Defence segment produces and sells products which have an application in defence-related
activities. Forged components used in defence-related activities and products are included as a part of the Forgings segment.
