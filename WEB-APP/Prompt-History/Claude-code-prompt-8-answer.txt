## Summary

- **Tax liabilities on the Balance Sheet fell sharply.** Deferred tax liabilities (net) dropped from ₹1,198.28 mn to ₹215.24 mn, down 82.0%. Current tax liabilities (net) were almost flat at ₹393.16 mn (Balance Sheet, p.341). Together, tax liabilities are now only about 0.5% of total liabilities, down from about 1.5%. The fall in deferred tax liabilities was **not driven by the P&L**. It came mainly from a **₹1,338.69 mn deferred tax credit on cash flow hedge losses** taken through OCI (Note 21, p.407).
- **The group's net deferred tax position swung further into an asset.** Net deferred tax assets rose from ₹703.15 mn to ₹1,906.75 mn. About ₹1,117.70 mn of this now depends on the reversal of **hedge losses** (Note 21, p.406), which is an accounting-driven asset rather than a structural one.
- **The effective tax rate is high: 34.66%** (prior year 37.27%), against a statutory rate of 25.168%. The gap comes mainly from **₹1,426.83 mn of tax on losses for which no deferred tax asset is recognised**. Without it the rate would be about 26.1% in both years. Carried-forward losses grew to ₹29,399.75 mn, led by **Germany (+43.6%)** (Note 21, p.405–407).
- **Current tax is backed by cash.** Taxes paid of ₹5,646.25 mn are about 100.7% of the current tax charge of ₹5,606.07 mn (Cash Flow, p.346; P&L, p.342). There is no sign of cash taxes building up as unpaid liabilities.
- **The note has several internal inconsistencies:**
  - The deferred tax components sum to ₹138.67 mn, not the ₹171.67 mn reported. The ₹33.00 mn difference is unexplained.
  - The prior-year split of OCI tax is wrong.
  - The prior-year tax adjustment in the rate reconciliation has the opposite sign to the P&L figure.
  - The gross DTA/DTL presentation is mislabelled.

## Detailed analysis

### 1. Composition of tax liabilities

**Current tax liability**
- Current tax liabilities (net) of ₹393.16 mn are small against an annual current tax charge of ₹5,606.07 mn. That is about 7% of the charge, or roughly 25 days' worth.
- Advance tax and withholding broadly cover the charge.
- The group is in a **net current-tax receivable position** of ₹112.19 mn (505.35 − 393.16), up from ₹12.30 mn.
- A simple roll-forward (the operating net current-tax asset, plus taxes paid, minus the current tax charge) is: 12.30 + 5,646.25 − 5,606.07 = **52.48**. That is ₹59.71 mn short of the actual ₹112.19 mn. The gap is probably due to:
  - the subsidiary acquired during the year;
  - foreign exchange; and
  - offsetting between entities.
- These items are not disclosed.

**Deferred tax liability (net) of ₹215.24 mn.** Components (Note 21, p.406):

**Main driver: cash flow hedges.**
- The combined hedge-related deferred tax position swung from a liability of ₹188.01 mn to an asset of ₹1,117.70 mn. That is a move of ₹1,305.71 mn.
- This mirrors the pre-tax hedge loss of ₹5,312.67 mn in OCI.
- The tax on it is ₹1,338.69 mn, which is 25.2% of 5,312.67, broadly the statutory rate.
- **The fall in DTL is therefore mainly a mark-to-market effect, not a real reduction in tax exposure.** If the hedges reverse, the balances will swing back.

**Exceptional items:**
- Exceptional items of ₹1,544.43 mn add ₹210.20 mn of tax for non-deductible items.

## Red flags & areas for follow-up

1. **The deferred tax components do not foot to the P&L charge.**
   - The components give 120.74 + 224.55 − 206.62 = **138.67**, against the ₹171.67 mn reported (Note 21, p.406).
   - The DTL/DTA reconciliation gives the same figure: −143.07 + 281.74 = **138.67** (p.407).
   - That leaves an **unexplained ₹33.00 mn**. The prior year ties (−423.04).
   - The gap is close to the ₹32.98 mn difference between the hedge DTA/DTL movement (1,305.71) and the hedge OCI tax (1,338.69). This may be a misclassification.
2. **The prior-year OCI tax split is wrong.**
   - The note's prior-year items give −267.78 − 46.81 − 5.57 = −320.16, against the stated total of (309.02).
   - The prior-year FVTOCI tax should probably be **+5.57**, which would make it tie.
   - In the current year, the note's split (DBP 59.85 credit, FVTOCI 71.58 charge) conflicts with the SOCE-implied split (DBP 66.58 charge, FVTOCI 54.85 credit). The signs are also illogical: a DBP *gain* is shown with a tax credit, and an FVTOCI *loss* with a tax charge. Only the net 11.73 agrees.
3. **The prior-year tax line in the rate reconciliation has an inconsistent sign.**
   - The rate reconciliation shows "reversal of tax expenses of earlier years" as **+130.06**.
   - The P&L component shows prior-year tax as a **credit of (95.34)**.
   - The prior year is consistent at (146.26) in both places. Management should explain the ₹225.40 mn gap.
4. **The "Reflected in the balance sheet" table is mislabelled and inconsistent.**
   - In the DTA group, "Deferred tax liabilities" of **(1,042.68)** is negative, i.e. a debit.
   - The gross DTL in the DTL group (1,493.56) equals only the PPE line. It excludes ROU (623.31) and FVTOCI (181.57), which total 2,298.44.
   - The prior-year gross DTL of 1,790.98 does not agree with the components either (2,716.42).
5. **Some component movements do not match the P&L charge.** PPE deferred tax balances *fell* by ₹120.74 mn in total, yet the P&L shows a *charge* of ₹120.74 mn. The "Other temporary differences" and "Other adjustments" (+468.28 / −540.46) lines are large and vague. A detailed breakdown should be requested.
6. **The DTA depends heavily on hedge outcomes.** About ₹1.12 bn of DTA depends on hedge losses. Management should confirm recoverability and the expected reversal pattern.
7. **Germany's losses are rising fast.** They grew by ₹4.23 bn, and no DTA is recognised. Ask about the turnaround plan, the risk of goodwill or asset impairment, and whether any DTA is recognised on the Indian 8-year losses (₹4.40 bn) or US losses. Also ask why Swedish losses fell by ₹2.36 bn.
8. **Income tax assets and current tax liabilities have no supporting note.** Neither line carries a note reference and no breakdown was provided. The current-tax roll-forward leaves ₹59.71 mn unexplained. Uncertain tax positions and contingent tax disputes are not covered in the material provided.
9. **Typos and rounding:**
   - Germany's prior-year loss is printed as "₹9.698.51 million".
   - The prior-year ETR is stated as 37.26%; the computed figure is 37.27%.
   - The US losses "available for twenty years" are said to expire "between tax years 2026 and 2037", which should be clarified.
   - The reconciliation label "Tax (expense) during the period" is attached to a credit.
